About

About Me

I am Vishwas Saini, a working Video Editor and Cinematographer with a strong interest in cinematic storytelling, visual composition, creative direction, and high-quality video production.

In editing, I shape pacing, transitions, color, and sound so footage feels intentional. On camera, I use framing, lighting, and movement to support the story before a cut is made. Together, those choices help a sequence communicate mood and meaning rather than just showing what happened.

I work in video editing and cinematography, and I am building a portfolio of cinematic videos, edited pieces, short-form content, and camera work. Project examples will appear here as verified videos and links are added.
